# Sprint Parallel

Use this the moment a sprint has a **defined reset or cut time**. Do not wait to be reminded. This skill overrides serial “one department at a time” and the “GMs-room-only” habit for the duration of that sprint.

Outside a timed sprint, follow [Loop Corp](../../agent-operations/loop-corp/SKILL.md).

## Dual channel (same beat)

Grok Bot talks **both** in the same cycle, never one then the other:

1. **1:1 to each GM** — one concrete next action (not an ack).
2. **Dedicated rooms** — GM room + each GM’s staff room + each live workspace room.

GMs then talk to **every** specialist in their room in that same beat. Bots may 1:1 each other. Do **not** DM scouts from Grok Bot.

Idle / only-ack in any lane is a bug.

## Maximize parallelization

- All GMs and all workspaces move **now**, not in sequence.
- Do not wait for one map, one hallazgo, or one GM before starting the others.
- Prefer many small simultaneous loops over one deep serial thread.
- Self-classify every loop by **workspace · process · objective · skill**.
- If a bot is in the wrong room or duplicating a live role, say so and stop. Do not invent a third format.

## Pass-a-pass (value first)

Each live area maps **one** end-to-end process that actually generates value:

`trigger → skill → output → owner` (4 boxes)

Skip theater. Skip processes that do not return ROI, learning, or a stable day. Brand/workspace loops outrank generic org chatter when those workspaces are the north star.

Formats stay [hallazgo](../../agent-operations/hallazgo/SKILL.md) and [reporte-gm](../../agent-operations/reporte-gm/SKILL.md) only.

## Fill gaps (high quality margin)

As maps land, create or update **only** when the hole is real and quality is high:

- Agent: GM proposes, Grok Bot creates, no overlap with a live role.
- Frozen unless the human unlocks: Closer, Ventas, Partnerships, publishers, identity farm, Skool/WhatsApp, caja-bot.
- Docs (roster, org, contracts, comm graph) update in the same cycle as the change.
- Routines, skills, and rules stay **non-invasive**: weekday/daytime defaults, no scout spam, no extra fields, no new DBs.

A structural or A3 change waits for the human. Everything else proceeds.

## HITL later

The human reviews **one by one** at the cut (results and decisions). Do not dump wiring into that 1:1. Do not pause the parallel machine waiting for that review.

## Cut

At reset time: one quality corte (real numbers, links, n/d if missing). Then migrate what already exists. Do not open a new serial queue.

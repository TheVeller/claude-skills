# Campaign publish checklist

1. Open the campaign row in the Notion publishing database.
2. Check caption, image or media, channels, date and product. Ask the owning agent for anything missing: content draft, design, or calendar.
3. Propose the schedule. Default launch time is 00:01 Pacific Time; convert to the owner's local zone and note US daylight saving.
4. Do not fire any webhook or Notion button until the owner says yes in chat for that exact campaign.
5. After a publish or dry-run, write status, published URLs and real metrics back to the row and report to the brand manager.
6. Never invent connector webhooks. Never open secrets.
